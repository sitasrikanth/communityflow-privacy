CommunityFlow — Delete Your Account

Last updated: September 17, 2026

This page explains how to request deletion of your CommunityFlow account and the data associated with it, as required by Google Play's account deletion policy.

How to request deletion

  1. Preferred: contact your community's admin. Since CommunityFlow accounts are scoped to a specific residential community (apartment/society), your community's admin can deactivate and delete your account directly from within the app.
  2. Or, contact us directly at sitasrikanth@gmail.com. Include:
    • The phone number your account is registered under
    • The name of your community (society/apartment complex)
    We will verify your identity and process the deletion request, or route it to your community's admin, within a reasonable timeframe.

What gets deleted

DataDeleted?
Name, phone number, profile photoDeleted
Flat / Wing / Block address on fileDeleted
Login credentials (Quick Code)Deleted — account can no longer log in
Visitor entry records you're associated withDeleted
Feedback / complaints you submittedDeleted

What may be retained

Some records may be retained by your community even after your individual account is deleted, for legitimate accounting and record-keeping purposes:

  • Financial contribution / donation / expense records — retained as part of your community's event fund accounting history (e.g. "Flat 302 contributed ₹1,000 to Ganesh Chaturthi 2026"), since removing these would break the transparent fund records other residents and the community rely on. These records are retained without your live account/login attached to them once deleted.

If you'd like these financial records removed too (rather than retained anonymously), mention this explicitly in your deletion request — this is at your community admin's discretion, since it affects their accounting records.
