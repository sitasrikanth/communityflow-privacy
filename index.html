GateFlow — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 13, 2026

GateFlow ("the App", "we", "us") is a residential community management app used by apartment/society admins, residents, and security guards. This policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used, and your choices — written to match exactly what the App does today, not a generic template.

1. Who this applies to

GateFlow is used within a specific residential community after that community's admin or a Super Admin has set it up. You typically get access via an invitation/registration flow from your society's admin, not by signing up independently.

We do not collect precise location, contacts list, camera/microphone access beyond photo selection, browsing history, or any data unrelated to the features above.

3. How your information is stored

All data is stored using Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Firebase Cloud Messaging, and Firebase Storage), hosted on Google Cloud infrastructure. Data for your community is logically separated from other communities using GateFlow.

4. Who can see your information

  • Your community's admin can see resident/guard profiles, visitor logs, and financial records for their own community only.
  • Other residents may see limited information depending on the feature — e.g. a community notice board, or a shared contribution/donation status — never your phone number or private profile details beyond what a feature explicitly displays.
  • Security guards see visitor-log and shift-related information needed to do their job.
  • We (the developer) do not access your personal data except as needed to operate, maintain, or debug the App, or if legally required.

We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers. The App contains no third-party advertising SDK.

5. Data retention & deletion

Your data is retained as long as your account remains active within your community. To request deletion of your account and associated data, contact your community's admin or reach us directly at sitasrikanth@gmail.com. Some records (e.g. financial contribution history) may be retained by your community for accounting/record-keeping purposes even after an individual account is deactivated.

6. Children's privacy

GateFlow is not directed at children under 13, and account creation requires a valid phone number capable of receiving an OTP. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

7. Your choices

  • You can update your profile name/photo at any time within the App.
  • You can choose to submit feedback anonymously (visible only to your admin).
  • You can request account/data deletion by contacting your community admin or us directly.

8. Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we will update the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the App after a change means you accept the revised policy.
